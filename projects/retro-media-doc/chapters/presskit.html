MULTIMEDIA PRESS KITS when film promotion came on a CD-ROM.

Chapter 04 — Multimedia Press Kits

Long before the internet press kit, movie studios promoted films to journalists with physical kits: a folder of stills, bios and a synopsis. In the mid-1990s some studios upgraded to multimedia CD-ROM press kits — interactive, runnable presentations that were themselves a small piece of software. A notable surviving example is the 1997 DreamWorks "The Peacemaker" press kit, preserved and playable on the Internet Archive.

What a CD-ROM press kit was

ElementWhy it mattered
Autoplay / runnable presentationA journalist could pop the disc in a computer (often Windows/DOS) and browse the film's story and credits without a projector or print set.
Stills, synopsis, cast & crewThe same core content as a paper kit, but navigable and updatable.
Novelty / memoryA sleek disc read as "this studio is modern" — and was small enough to file or mail.

The Peacemaker (1997)

The Peacemaker press kit on the Internet Archive is a preserved, emulatable example of the form: a studio-distributed interactive promo disc from the late 90s, playable in a DOSBox emulator in the browser. It's a snapshot of the moment film marketing met multimedia computing — before websites became the default home for press materials.

Why this belongs in a documentary about "secrets of the screen": these kits are a forgotten middle step between paper press folders and the modern online press kit. They also connect directly to the little "electronic press kit" programs that shipped on floppy for 1992 films — promotional software as a curiosity of its era.

The earlier wave: electronic press kits on floppy

The CD-ROM kits descended from an even earlier experiment: film "electronic press kits" distributed on floppy disk around 1992 (notably tied to the tech-minded thriller Sneakers). Those were small DOS programs — a title screen, a hidden access code, decrypted text — a promotional toy that rewarded curious journalists. You can read a detailed reverse-engineering study of one such floppy in the research notes elsewhere in this repository.
