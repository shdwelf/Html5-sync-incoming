# Greeran "two Bernards" — reconciliation notes

**Prepared 2026-09-07.** Follow-up to `GREERAN_AHN17_RESEARCH_MEMO.md`. The project
labels two men "Ahn. 16 / Bernard" across sessions; this resolves who each is and
flags a generation wrinkle found in the Drumgooland Lower register transcription.

## The two Bernards in the compiled pedigree
```
Bernard Greeran (elder, ~1831–1908)            Ahn.16  × Mary Conalty (~1835–1913) Ahn.17
  └─ m. 28 Apr 1854, Drumgooland Lower (Decomet)
      ├─ John  bpt 25 May 1855
      ├─ Rose  bpt 22 Jan 1858
      ├─ Bernard (Jr)  bpt 18 Jul 1860  ← the "Ahn.16" the session-10 tree pairs w/ Conalty's family
      │     × Mary Hillan → father of Hugh Greeran (1896–1978)
      └─ Hugh   bpt 17 Jun 1863   ← a SECOND Hugh, brother of Bernard Jr
```
From the Rosalind Davies transcription of Drumgooland Lower (Rootsweb, the source
the project already trusts for the Doherty line), **Bernard Greenan of Decomet ×
Mary Conalty, m. 28 Apr 1854**, had sons **Bernard (bpt 18 Jul 1860)** and
**Hugh (bpt 17 Jun 1863)**. The 1854 date matches the repo's Ahn.17 exactly.

## Key reconciliations
1. **The 1860 baptism = the "Bernard Jr"** the completed tree places as father of
   Hugh Greeran (1896–1978). So elder Bernard (Ahn.16, b.~1831) + Mary Conalty is
   confirmed at the register level for their son Bernard Jr.
2. **The Hugh baptised 1863 is NOT Hugh Greeran (1896–1978).** He is Bernard Jr's
   *brother* (an uncle), not his son. The two are two generations apart. If any
   repo text implies the 1863 Hugh is the 1896 man, that is a conflation to correct.
3. **Ahn.16 elder Bernard's own baptism is not in Drumgooland Lower** — that
   register's baptisms start 24 Mar 1832, and he was born ~1831 (just before start).
   That is consistent with Session 8's inability to find his baptism and is *not*
   an error.
4. **The single real gap remains** the *mother's maiden name on elder Bernard
   (~1831)'s own birth* — pre-1832, outside this register — or, alternatively, his
   parents' 1820s marriage (in the Drumgooland marriage register from 21 Jan 1833,
   or a neighbouring parish). Nothing further was found this session.
