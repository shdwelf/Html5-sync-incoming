# Greeran Ahn. 17 — gap-closure research memo

**Prepared 2026-09-07.** Aim: help close the one named "blocked" gap in the
Greeran Seize Quartiers project — identifying the mother of the Bernard Greeran
generation baptised in Drumgooland Lower, Co. Down, ~1860–63 — using the register
transcriptions the project already trusts, without fabricating records.

---

## 1. Fresh corroborating evidence (not previously cited in the repo)

The **Rosalind Davies surname transcriptions on Rootsweb** (a source this project
already relies on for the Doherty/Dogherty family) transcribe the Drumgooland
Lower Catholic register as follows (surname spelled *Greenan* by the priest):

> **Bernard GREENAN, of Drumgooland, of Decomet** — married **Mary Conalty**
> **28 Apr 1854** at Drumgooland Lower Catholic Church; father of
> **John bpt. 25 May 1855**, **Rose bpt. 22 Jan 1858**,
> **Bernard bpt. 18 Jul 1860**, **Hugh bpt. 17 Jun 1863** at Drumgooland Lower.
> *(Rootsweb — sites.rootsweb.com/~rosdavies/SURNAMES/G/Gre.htm)*

### Why this matters
- The marriage date **28 Apr 1854** is byte-for-byte the date the repo's
  breakthrough doc already assigns to **Ahn. 17 = Mary Conalty** ("m. 28 Apr 1854,
  Drumgooland", PRF QVVM-QLL). So this is an **independent, register-derived
  confirmation** of that identity and marriage.
- It supplies exact **baptism dates** for the two Bernard-generation sons —
  **Bernard bpt. 18 Jul 1860** and **Hugh bpt. 17 Jun 1863** — which lets anyone
  locate and verify them on the actual NLI pages (see §3).

Across sessions the project's "Ahn. 17" label points at two *different* questions:

| Session framing | Question | Status in repo |
|---|---|---|

These are compatible only if the "Bernard Sr." needing a mother-search in Session 8
is a **different man** from the Ahn. 16 Bernard (b. ~1831) already paired with Mary
Conalty. Note: Drumgooland Lower **baptisms begin 24 Mar 1832**, so a Bernard born
~1831 would *barely fall inside or just before* the register — which is likely why
Session 8 could not find his baptism, while the *son* Bernard (bpt 1860) is
findable. Decide explicitly which Bernard fills Ahn. 16 before treating this closed.

## 3. Verification protocol (5 minutes, human, free) — to truly close it

Open the register at the NLI, then check the named baptism rows by eye:

- Register: Drumgooland Lower Catholic registers, **NLI vtls000633272**,
  Microfilm 05497/05, baptisms 24 Mar 1832 – 18 May 1881 (176 images).
  URL: `https://registers.nli.ie/registers/vtls000633272`
- **Bernard bpt. 18 Jul 1860** → ~**page 102–103** (page 103 covers Dec 1860–May 1861).
- **Hugh bpt. 17 Jun 1863** → ~**page 104–105**.
- Look for: child **Bernardus / Bernard Greenan**, father **Bernardus Greenan**,
  mother **Maria Conalty** (register Latin), townland **Decomet**,
  sponsors (often siblings/relatives) — sponsors can extend the tree.

If the eye-check confirms "Maria Conalty" as mother on the 1860 (and/or 1863) row,
then **Ahn. 17 = Mary Conalty is confirmed at the primary-source level**, and the
only genuinely open question becomes the *father's* own parents (the Ahn. 16
Bernard b.~1831), which fall at/just-before the register's 1832 start and would
need the 1820s Drumgooland or surrounding-parish registers or PRF.
