# Greeran family — proposed ORIGINAL coat of arms (new design)

**Important framing:** These are **newly designed arms** for the family, **not**
a historical coat of arms and **not** an official grant. There is no "the Greeran
crest" you can buy; authentic arms attach to individuals. If you ever want a
*legal* grant, apply to the appropriate heraldic authority (Ireland: Genealogical
Office / Chief Herald of Ireland; England & Wales: College of Arms; etc.) and they
would start from a design much like this. This document gives you a proper,
heraldically-formatted design whose symbolism comes from the **documented** family
story in `GREERAN_HERALDRY.md`.

---

## 1. Proposed blazon (heraldic description)

> **Azure, a fess wavy Argent between in chief three mullets Argent and in base
> a garb Or.**

**Crest (on helm):** *A cinquefoil Argent charged with a mullet Azure.*
**Motto (primary, English):** *"Through work and water."*
**Motto (optional, Irish):** *"Trí shaothar agus farraige"* — "through labour and sea."

---

## 2. Symbolism (each charge maps to a documented fact)

| Element | Tincture/charge | Meaning drawn from the documented line |
|---|---|---|
| Field | **Azure** (blue) | The Atlantic crossed in emigration (Co. Down → Boston/New York → California), and the navy service (US Navy, WWII). |
| Fess | **wavy Argent** (silver wave) | The sea journey itself; the "wave" the family rode in each generation's emigration. |
| Three mullets (stars) | **Argent** | Three generations of documented military service: **Samuel Carey** (Revolutionary War, 1780–81), the **WWII** veterans (US Navy/ Army), and **CPL Greeran** (USMC, Vietnam). Also, standing in chief, they read as "leading the way." |
| Garb (sheaf of wheat) | **Or** (gold) | The Drumgooland / **Decomet** tenant-farming root of the Irish line (Bernard Greeran, farmer), the generation from which everything descends. |
| Crest cinquefoil | Argent/Azure | A leaf motif standing for the five named Co. Down townlands of the core generations (Ballymacarney, Decomet, Gargory, Ballymagreehan, Magheramayo); charged with a mullet to tie the military service into the crest. |

**Why these and not a harp/shield/leprechaun "Irish family crest" cliché:** the
charges above are specific to *this* family's documented geography and service,
whereas generic surname-market shields are not attached to any real ancestor here.

## 4. Honest cautions (read before using anywhere formal)
1. This is a **design proposal**, not an entitlement — display it as a family emblem,
   not as "the historical Greeran coat of arms."
2. Do **not** claim noble or armigerous status from the seize-quartiers genealogy;
   the documented ancestors are commoners (that's a *lineage-completeness*
   achievement, not nobility).
3. If you want legal protection/exclusivity, register through the Chief Herald's
   office (Ireland) — there it is standard practice to *grant new* arms, which is
   exactly the legitimate path this design serves.
