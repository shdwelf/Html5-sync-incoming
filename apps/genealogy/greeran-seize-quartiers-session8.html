🔒 Ahn. 17 — The Final Frontier

Why Ahn. 17 Cannot Be Found Automatically

Bernard Greeran Sr. was born approximately 1861-1863 in Drumgooland parish, Co. Down. Civil registration started in Ireland in 1864 — so he missed the civil records entirely. His only record is his RC baptism in the NLI Drumgooland Lower register (free to view in browser at registers.nli.ie). The NLI images cannot be fetched programmatically (HTTP 500 without valid browser session cookies). All commercial indexers (FindMyPast, Ancestry, rootsireland.ie) have either missed this entry or require subscription.

⚡ How to Solve Ahn. 17 — Three Actionable Steps
  • Option 1 — FREE (5 minutes): Open registers.nli.ie/registers/vtls000633272#page/104/mode/1up in any browser. Use the page slider or filter to navigate to 1861-1863. Look for a Greenan/Greeran entry with father Bernard. The baptism entry will give: child's name, baptism date, father's name (Bernard Greeran), father's townland, mother's maiden name (= Ahn.17 answer). This is completely free.
  • Option 2 — Paid (~£5): Purchase a search at rootsireland.ie for "Greeran OR Greenan" in Drumgooland lower baptisms 1829-1922. The Ulster Historical Foundation has this indexed and the result will include parents' names.
  • Option 3 — Alternative approach: Search the NLI Drumgooland Lower MARRIAGE register for "Bernard Greeran" (his parents would appear there too if he married before 1880 — but he married in 1894, after the NLI register ends). However, searching for the marriage of his PARENTS in Drumgooland Lower (which covers 1832-1880) might name his mother.
