SECRETS OF THE SCREEN the arcade, the scene, the hidden messages, and the electronic press kit — how games & movies hid their inner workings in plain sight.

Four original web chapters about things hidden in software and media: the coin-op arcade's history, the crack-intro & demoscene, software easter eggs, and the forgotten multimedia press kits that studios shipped on CD-ROM in the 1990s. Every animation and note on this site is generated live in your browser — no media files are reproduced.

How to read this site. Use the top menu to move between chapters (they are separate pages), press ♪ music for a synthesised soundtrack, and open each canvas preview to appreciate the live effects. Content is original, written from general public-history research; sources are listed at the foot of each chapter and point onward to the great public archives (Museum of the Game, the Easter Egg Archive, the Internet Archive) where the originals live.
A note on the scene chapter. The crack-intro & demoscene are celebrated here strictly as a historical art form. This site distributes no cracked software, keygens, or copied artwork — the genuine originals are preserved by scene archives and the Internet Archive, and links point there rather than reproducing their files.
